About

I learn by building the thing, breaking it, and writing down what happened. That is the whole method, and it has outlasted every tutorial I have ever started.

Twenty-five, studying IT in Germany. The lab is where the theory gets tested: a server, switching, firewall rules, and a segmented network I can explain line by line. Security is the other half — mostly the unglamorous part, the enumeration, the notes, the second look.

I work in English, think in German, and prefer a short conversation to a long specification.
